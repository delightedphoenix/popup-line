# Roadmap

What comes after slice 1, in order.

1. **Slice 1: the line** (planned, not built yet). Scan a QR code, enter a first name, see your live place in line; Lexie calls the next guest from her phone. See [build-brief-slice-1.md](build-brief-slice-1.md).
2. **Slice 2: text alerts.** A text when a guest is two spots away. This needs the guest's phone number and their OK to be texted. Dashboard actions should also move behind Firebase Auth at this point.
3. **Gallery delivery.** A private gallery link sent to each guest after the event.
4. **Tips and socials.** Ways for guests to leave a tip and find Lexie Wilcox Photography's social accounts.
